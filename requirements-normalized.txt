--- PAGE 1 --- AEA FINANCE PLATFORM Complete Product Requirements, Business Rules, and Implementation Guide Ateneo Economics Association (AEA) - Office of the Chief Financial Officer (OCFO) DOCUMENT PURPOSE This is the source-of-truth specification for an IDE AI agent building the internal AEA Finance website. Follow the product rules, permissions, workflows, calculations, storage design, security rules, and implementation order below. Do not invent conflicting behavior. When a detail is not yet finalized, make it configurable instead of hard-coding it. PRIMARY GOAL Build a reusable finance operations platform that future OCFO teams can inherit. FINANCIAL RULES Approved Budget • Initial approved department amount. • Can change only through approved adjustments. Current Approved Budget = Initial Approved Budget + Approved Budget Adjustments Committed Funds • Approved requests not yet fully realized as actual expense. • Reduce available funds immediately. • Rejected/cancelled requests must not remain committed. Actual Expenses • Any completed/recorded expense transaction. Available Funds Available Funds = Current Approved Budget - Actual Expenses - Active Committed Funds Revenue • Track separately from expenses. • Do not silently net revenue against expenses in the ledger. • Net financial impact may be a derived dashboard metric. When an actual expense is recorded against a commitment: • decrease remaining commitment • increase actual expenses • if actual < approved commitment, release unused balance when completed • if actual > approved commitment, create critical discrepancy unless approved adjustment exists Department budget is always authoritative; project is analytical only. --- PAGE 16 --- 12.
